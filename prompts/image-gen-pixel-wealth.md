# Image prompts — 19 Pixel Wealth rooms

Purpose: the exact prompts that made the Pixel Wealth rooms, plus the template for new rooms.
Source: AIVideo project "Final CCE Directions" `29c40ecf12724d03bec9c2e5cada7c31` (prompts read back from the generated assets). Last updated: 2026-11-25.

**Model:** GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (`gpt-image-2-6-sunburst`), 1:1, Standard, about 7 credits each. **Then:** retrace each room onto the pixel grid (`motion-kit/tools/pixel-retrace.py room.png out.png 216 40`) and check it at 4x. Playbook: `motion-kit/styles/19-pixel-wealth.md`.

## Template for a new room
```
Pixel art in the style of Habbo Hotel: an isometric <ROOM> seen from above at the classic 2:1 isometric angle, <one room corner with two walls | as a floating diamond-shaped tile>. <5–10 concrete objects that tell the story, placed around the edges>. Palette: <4–6 colours, keep royal blue, cream, warm gold>. Crisp hand-placed pixels, clean 1-pixel dark outlines on furniture, flat colour areas with 2-3 tone shading, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no blur. Empty room, no people, no characters, no text, no letters, no logos, no currency symbols, no money signs. The room fills the frame, centred, with open floor space in the middle.
```
Keep "open floor space in the middle": characters must stand there. Add "no numbers" when a room has screens or frames.
