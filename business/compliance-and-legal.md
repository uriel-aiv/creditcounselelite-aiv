## Ad-writing guardrails (derived)
- Never promise approval, specific amounts, score increases or timelines → use "up to", "members have", "results vary"
- Don't call CCE "credit repair" or imply CCE gets the funding for you
- Pair any testimonial number with a results-vary line
- Meta personal-attributes rule: avoid "your bad credit", "are you in debt?" — speak about the topic, not the viewer's condition
- "0% interest" = intro APR periods on cards; be careful not to imply loans/free money
