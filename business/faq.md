# FAQ & Objection Handling

Source: /home, /how-it-works · Last updated: 2026-11-19

| Question / objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| What makes CCE different? | Funders charge big fees every round. CCE teaches you to run the system yourself every 6 months per person and keep the upside. |
| How fast can I get funded? | Some in 10 days; most 30–90 days; current avg ~72–80 days. Repeatable every 6 months. |
| I've been denied before. | Common. CCE identifies what's holding the profile back and what lenders look for. |
| Do I need a high score? | No perfect credit needed. 650+ FICO is the strongest typical start; age, utilization, accounts, inquiries, negatives, income and bank positioning all reviewed. |
| Business credit / non-PG? | Yes — Inner Circle: advanced business credit, vetted non-PG pathways where appropriate, 160+ tradeline resources. |
| Time commitment? | ~30 min/day (5 h/wk) at first; ~2 h/wk after 90 days. |
| Spouse/partner? | Yes; both must attend all coaching calls. |
| What if banks don't approve me? | Approvals never guaranteed. You get 9,500+ banks list and learn the 45+ underwriting metrics. Lenders decide. |
| Who is this NOT for? | Shortcut seekers, done-for-you seekers, non-coachable, anyone planning to default on banks. |
| Which program? | Decided on the free Credit Assessment Call. |
| What can I use funds for? | Business start/scale, real estate, marketing, income streams; first invites to investments. |
| Next steps? | Assessment call → enroll → agreement → Welcome Call (≤2 days) → meet team → Roadmap Kick-Off (90-day plan). |
| Why not a funding company? | They charge 10–30% backend per round ($5K–$15K on $50K), can cause hard inquiries/score damage, teach nothing repeatable. |
