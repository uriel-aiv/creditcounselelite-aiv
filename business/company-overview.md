## What CCE is
An **education and coaching company** that teaches entrepreneurs, business owners and real estate investors how to build a bank-ready credit profile, access large amounts of 0% interest capital (personal + business credit), and leverage it into businesses, real estate and wealth — as a **repeatable skill** (every 6 months, per person, spouse/partner included).

## What CCE is NOT
- Not a credit repair organization
- Not a lender or loan broker
- Does not dispute items, contact creditors, or apply for credit on members' behalf
- Lenders alone decide approvals; results vary

## Target audience
- Entrepreneurs & business owners wanting capital to start/scale
- Real estate investors (flips, rentals, BRRRR, ground-up)
- W2 employees transitioning into investing
- Couples / spouses (both can run the playbook)

**Not for:** shortcut seekers, people wanting done-for-you, non-coachable people, anyone planning to default on banks.
**Typical starting point:** 650+ FICO (full profile reviewed).
**Geo restriction:** Georgia residents not accepted; Alaska & Hawaii limited (per earnings disclaimer).

## Differentiators
- **No percentage backend fees** (competitors charge 10–30% per round — e.g. $5K–$15K on $50K)
- **Repeatable playbook** — run it every 6 months, teach it to spouse/partner
- **Personalized strategy** built on the member's profile, goals and bank fit
- **Banking relationships + 9,500-bank database**
- **Proprietary "Mass Apply" sequencing strategy**
- **Access beyond capital:** first invites to founder-led deals (e.g. San Diego ground-up construction), network for tax, bookkeeping, trusts, asset protection, insurance
- **Community + accountability**
