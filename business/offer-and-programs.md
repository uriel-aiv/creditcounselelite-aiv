# Offer & Programs

Source: /apply, /how-it-works, /home · Last updated: 2026-11-19

## Core promise
Get up to **$500K+ in 0% interest funding** to scale your business or real estate portfolio, using the repeatable **0% Capital Playbook** — with **zero percentage backend fees**. First funding goals of $5K, $20K, up to $500K; members build toward $200K–$500K rounds.

## Funnel
1. **Watch the Free Training** (0% Capital Playbook, 3 positioning steps, 45+ metrics, common mistakes, programs)
2. **Book a Free 15-Minute Intro Call** / Complimentary Credit Assessment Call with an Education Coach (credit, funding goals, use of capital)
3. **Enroll** in the right program → sign agreement
4. **Welcome Call** within 2 days → meet the expert team
5. **Roadmap Kick-Off Call** → 90-day plan
6. **Fund, leverage, repeat** every 6 months
