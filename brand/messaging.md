2. **Repeatable skill** — every 6 months, for you and your spouse
3. **From zero to $14M** — founders' restaurant-jobs-to-portfolio story
4. **Proof** — $370K+ avg, 4,000+ members, real member wins
5. **Access beyond capital** — deals, network, travel, community
6. **Education, not shortcuts** — coachable, disciplined, action-driven
