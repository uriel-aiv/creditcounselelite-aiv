# 17 Brand Native — style playbook

Purpose: make a new clip in the 17 Brand Native style at the approved quality.
Source: rounds 1–3 of the CCE code motion tests. Last updated: 2026-11-25.

**Latest films:** `films/cce-ft17-brandnative` (VSL #4, v03) and `films/cce-r2-17-brandnative` (Video 13, v01). Copy the r2 film.

## The look
Built only from the CCE Brand Style Guidelines 2025: black ground with background B from `bg-tests/` (twisted light sheets of fine lines plus a dot band, at 45% opacity so content reads first; no bright core line); gold metal plaques with chamfered corners and bolts (like the metal logo) holding the key words and numbers; ANTON caps; white neon tube type for single key moments (NEXT DAY, 0%, 7–21X); white brush swooshes; members cards (no numbers) in black, blue and Playbook faces with gold edges; a gold seam line between motion and founder.

## Assets
None generated. Neon and brush are drawn in code (the real Have Heart Two and Neoneon fonts were not available; if the team supplies them, add to `fonts/` and register in `index.html`).

## Engine
`plaque`, `chamfer`, `goldGrad`, `neon`, `brush`, `card`, `beam`, `bank`, `lock`, `plane`, `person`, `strikePlaque`, background sheets (`sheet`, `drawLines`, `drawDots`, `ribbonPt`), scenes `sA…sG`, `LAYOUT`.

## Must
- All secondary words in Anton (tracking ~1px), light blue inside plaques.
- Titles in corporate colours only.

## Never
- Small letterspaced labels or labels between rules (removed on feedback), small step text at the top.
