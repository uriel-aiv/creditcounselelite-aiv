# 16 Board Game — style playbook

Purpose: rebuild or extend the 16 Board Game style (original style, explored, not chosen for footage tests).
Source: style round 1 of the CCE code motion tests. Last updated: 2026-11-25.

**Films:** `films/cce-board` (v03) and `films/cce-board-brand` (v03). Copy the brand film.

## The look
An original property-trading board game: a mint board ringed by spaces named for the script's ideas, colour title bars, corner squares; a navy pawn hops space to space on the spoken beats while the camera follows and the board turns at each corner; dice rolls, flip cards, green houses and a red hotel.

## Assets
Chance-style cards: landscape pale yellow/orange stock, "PLAYBOOK" header, bold geometric sans (Jost) title and footer on the left, plain black pen line drawings on the right (no fills, no shading) generated with GPT Image (transparent). Prompts: AIVideo project "CCE Art Direction".

## Must / Never
- Clearly original: no third-party names, tokens or mascots (no top-hat moustache gentleman).
- Play-money bills carry no numbers or currency symbols.
