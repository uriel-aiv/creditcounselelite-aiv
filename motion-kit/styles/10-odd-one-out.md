# 10 Odd One Out (Visualize Value) — style playbook

Purpose: make a new clip in the 10 Odd One Out style at the approved quality.
Source: rounds 1–3 of the CCE code motion tests. Last updated: 2026-11-25.

**Latest films:** `films/cce-ft10-oddone` (VSL #6, v03) and `films/cce-r2-10-oddone` (VSL #1, v01). Copy the r2 film.

## The look
Visualize Value: pure black, thin white geometric line diagrams drawn on stroke by stroke and morphing into the next. Each beat is a concept (containers, wedge, distribution, orbit, path, fan, exponential curve). Solid vs outline marks the one thing that matters. Strictly black and white: no brand or Playbook colours.

## Assets
None: all strokes in code (`lineOn`, `circleOn`, `rectOn`, `polyOn`, `path`, `bez`, `along`, `ripple`, `dotAt`).

## Must
- Only shape and movement metaphors: dots, circles, rings, lines, squares, grids, forks, paths, ripples.
- Small uppercase labels are allowed here (the one style where they were accepted), kept minimal.
- Graphics of separate ideas never overlap (several ideas may share one graphic if it stays legible).

## Never
- Objects or icons: no gauges, banks, megaphones, documents, check marks.
- Any colour.
